# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Workforce One serves Malaysian people administrators and employees. Administrators manage people, attendance, leave, payroll, and reports. Employees use a responsive self-service workspace for attendance, leave planning, notifications, profiles, and payslips.

## Product Purpose

Provide a credible, connected HR and payroll demonstration in which operational records flow from attendance and leave through payroll and protected payslips. Success means each screen helps a user complete or understand a real workflow using mutable, auditable data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and human. The product should feel dependable during consequential HR work without becoming cold or visually dense.

## Anti-references

Do not copy Workday, GajiKita, or another HR product's visual identity. Avoid decorative dashboard mosaics, excessive gradients, oversized cards, fake analytics, consumer-style gamification, and dense enterprise configuration screens.

## Design Principles

- Put the working surface before summary decoration.
- Make status, ownership, and the next action immediately legible.
- Reveal sensitive detail only to the role that needs it.
- Keep calculations traceable and administrative changes auditable.
- Preserve familiar controls and responsive employee workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, and mobile layouts down to 320px.
